# AGENTS.md

## What this is

Static single-page personal site (Linked Data CV) for Sebastian David Schiller-Stoff. There is no package manager, build, test, lint, or CI workflow; assets are served as plain files from the repo root. Origin is `Schiller-Stoff/Schiller-Stoff.github.io`; `master` is the published branch (GitHub Pages settings live outside the repo).

The published design is V4, “Evidence, clearly presented”: a neutral institutional palette, sans-led typography, and evidence-first hierarchy aimed at research directors, programme managers, cultural-heritage institutions and hiring managers. The `redesign` branch adds V5, “Living evidence”: one finite hero scene (typed sentence, linked-data graph, profile settle), one-pass structural rule draws, and restrained interaction feedback layered on the unchanged V4 structure. V6, “The page answers”, promotes the typed line to a rotating, pausable focus line, adds arrival acknowledgement, copy/submit feedback, a scroll-drawn CV spine and a footer graph bookend. V7, “Blue ink”, reskins the same structure and motion: a white canvas, one ultramarine accent, serif statements, pills and circles, and an ultramarine footer. V8, “Depth, restrained”, adds the page's only scroll-linked parallax (a 48px drift of the hero graph), an ultramarine fill on the register arrow, and `storymap.html` — a static, dependency-free data study of DERLA's 4,393 documented places of remembrance. V9, “The page shows its data”, promotes that study to the CV itself: a feature plate directly after the credibility strip with the real inline map, three counts and one pill, and retires V8's late bridge band. V10, “Two remembrance platforms”, adds MEMOR — the City of Graz's digital memorial book — as a peer exhibit immediately after the DERLA plate (same component, one hairline instead of a second accent rule), a second dependency-free page `memor.html` (“The Last Addresses”) with its own inline city map, one closing note that states the shared method, and a print-visible MEMOR dataset line. V11, “The practice, not the diary”, moves the top of the page from a CV to a commissionable practice without moving a section: the hero becomes a one-column offer with its conversion control and a named engagement line, the portrait moves into a paper profile band immediately after it, the credibility strip becomes affiliation · system · prize · procurement, and services and contact say what can be commissioned and how to start. V12, “The ways in”, replaces the hero's decorative linked-data graph with a solid ultramarine index window — four real routes, a white sash and one factual meta line each — in the same slot beside the copy; the V8 hero drift retires with the graph. V13, “Depth at the fold”, puts a printed-sheet field behind the hero — a 96px ledger grid, a ghost index card and two registration crosses at 7–30 % ink — and lets only that field drift (64/48/16px over the first 90vh), with a pointer light on the index panes and a drawn filter underline. `redesignv13.md` is the current record, `redesignv12.md` the previous record, `redesignv11.md` the record before that, `redesignv10.md` the V10 record, `redesignv9.md` the V9 record, `redesignv8.md` the motion and depth record, `storymap-draft.md` the storymap scope and data record, `redesignv7.md` the previous visual rationale, `redesignv6.md` the previous motion record, `redesignv5.md` the V5 record, `redesignv4.md` the structural rationale, `redesignv3.md` the previous direction and `redesignv2.md` / `REDESIGN-PLAN.md` are superseded history.

## Source of truth

- `index.html` (~1,750 lines): the whole page. Sections in order: hero (`#top`), profile band (`.profile-band`, `aria-label="Profile"`), credibility strip, storymap feature plate (`#storymap-feature`), MEMOR feature plate (`#memor-feature`), selected outcomes (`#case-studies-section`, with the `#announcements` alias), services/capabilities (`#services-section`), working method (`#approach-section`), project register (`#portfolio-section`), training (`#teaching-section`), CV (`#cv-section`), contact (`#contact-section`), imprint (`#imprint-section`), footer. All ids and the fragment links between them are part of the public contract — do not rename them. The two plates are one band: `#memor-feature` closes it with a single note.
- `storymap.html`: the first standalone storymap page — “The Memory Landscape”, a static data study of DERLA. Same fonts, tokens and stylesheet, its own masthead/footer, four chapters plus a colophon, an inline 19KB SVG map of all 4,393 records, and **no JavaScript at all**. Its own ids (`ch-01`…`ch-04`, `colophon`) are local to the page; it links back to `index.html#storymap-feature`, across to `memor.html` and out to DERLA.
- `memor.html`: the second standalone storymap page — “The Last Addresses”, a static data study of MEMOR, the City of Graz's digital memorial book. Same fonts, tokens and stylesheet, its own masthead/footer, a sticky chapter rail, five chapters on alternating grounds, a colophon and a small footer, plus one inline city map (9.7KB SVG, `memor:begin` / `memor:end` markers) and **no JavaScript at all**. Its ids (`ch-01`…`ch-05`, `colophon`) are local to the page; it links back to `index.html#memor-feature`, across to `storymap.html` and out to MEMOR. It prints as a report, like `storymap.html`.
- `css/custom-styles.css`: the only stylesheet and the de facto source. It starts with one `:root` token block (white canvas and surface, paper/bed/tint surfaces, ink, one ultramarine action plus positive/error, ultramarine-footer on-dark colours, two typefaces, six text sizes plus six display roles, five leading and three tracking roles, an eight-step 4px spacing scale, 0/2px/pill/circle radii, 1px/2px rules, motion). Motion tokens are `--dur-fast`/`--dur-base`/`--dur-draw`/`--dur-scene`/`--dur-arrive`/`--dur-sweep`/`--dur-press`, `--ease-standard`/`--ease-enter` and `--shift-sm` (3px)/`--shift-md` (6px); `--dur` and `--ease` remain aliases for V4 component transitions. Eighteen sections style the CV; §19 styles both standalone pages under `sm-*` (`storymap.html` and `memor.html` share it; its `map__*` artefact rules are deliberately shared with the CV plates); §20 forced colours, §21 motion, §22 print. Edit this file directly; do not add a second stylesheet.
- `js/site.js`: all behaviour, vanilla JavaScript, loaded with `defer`. Guarded modules: footer year, header scroll state, non-modal mobile navigation, scrollspy, project filters/load-more, Formspree form, rotating hero focus line, arrival acknowledgement, copy e-mail, decorative rule observer. Neither `storymap.html` nor `memor.html` loads any script at all — both pages have to be complete without one.
- `tools/storymap/build_map.py`: the offline map generator behind both map instances. Extracts the inline FeatureCollection from DERLA's public map page, filters malformed coordinates, projects to Albers, emits one SVG and splices it between the `map:begin` / `map:end` markers — `--splice` is repeatable, and one run must update `storymap.html` **and** `index.html` so the two maps cannot drift. Coincident points are deduplicated at whole viewBox units (4,393 records → 1,545 positions, visually identical). Run it by hand (`--map-html` / `--natural-earth` accept saved copies); its output is committed, like the WebP derivatives. Never run it at page load.
- `tools/memor/build_map.py`: the offline generator behind both MEMOR map instances — the CV plate and `memor.html`. Reads MEMOR's public EVENTS GeoJSON, keeps the last voluntary addresses inside a padded Graz bbox, projects local equirectangular (longitude × cos φ₀) into a 600×586 viewBox, and splices one SVG between the `memor:begin` / `memor:end` markers (`--splice` repeatable; one run must update `memor.html` **and** `index.html`). The boundary comes from the Nominatim polygon for OSM relation 34719 (ODbL), not the City of Graz's service-terms geodata. Coincident points are deduplicated at whole viewBox units (604 addresses → 584 positions) and emitted once. It also prints the study's counts and distance bins, and (with `--natural-earth`) the country split. Run it by hand (`--events` / `--boundary` / `--natural-earth` accept saved copies); its output is committed. Never run it at page load. See `redesignv10.md` §4 for why the forced addresses are a chapter, not a map layer.
- `fonts/`: two self-hosted OFL woff2 files — `ibm-plex-sans.woff2` (variable 400–600, the UI face) and `source-serif-4.woff2` (variable, the statement face: wordmark, `h1`, `h2` and the two editorial `h3` roles). No CDN fonts, no mono face.
- `images/`: WebP content assets plus `og-cover.jpg` for social previews. The profile-band portrait is `images/profile/sebastian-stoff.webp`.
- `redesignv13.md` is the current record: the hero scene (grid, ghost index card, registration marks), its scroll depth, the index pointer light, the filter underline and the implementation record. `redesignv12.md` is the previous record: the hero index window, its four routes, the retired V8 drift and the implementation record. `redesignv11.md` is the record before that: the hero offer, the profile band, the proof strip, the services/contact copy deck and the review log. `redesignv10.md` is the V10 record (the MEMOR plate, the city map, the one-dot-class decision, the editorial groupings, the data and licence record, and the implementation record). `redesignv9.md` is the V9 record (DERLA plate placement, the inline map, deduplication, the no-motion stance). `redesignv8.md` is the motion and depth record (hero graph drift, register arrow fill) with its review verdict and fallbacks. `storymap-draft.md` is the DERLA storymap's data, licence and editorial record; the Graz walk stays there as a deferred appendix. `redesignv7.md` is the previous visual rationale and implementation record. `redesignv6.md` is the previous motion record. `redesignv5.md` is the V5 motion record. `redesignv4.md` is the V4 structural rationale. `design-refactor.md` is the implemented design-system refactor: tokens, labels/tags, colour semantics, media derivatives, register density, filters and code cleanup. ## Editing gotchas

- **No build step.** Change `index.html`, `storymap.html`, `memor.html`, `css/custom-styles.css`, and `js/site.js` directly. Do not introduce a bundler, framework, package manager, or CDN dependency. `tools/storymap/build_map.py` and `tools/memor/build_map.py` are offline generators for one committed SVG fragment each, spliced into two hosts each, not a build pipeline.
- **No content reveal.** Content, images, evidence and controls are never hidden pending JavaScript or scroll; the only progressive-enhancement switch for controls is `html.js-ready`. Motion may gate **decorative ink only**: `html.motion-ready` (added by `initDrawnRules`) hides the evidence-band rule, the delivery-path rule, section eyebrow markers and the footer graph until an `IntersectionObserver` adds `.is-drawn`, and the observer unobserves each target after one pass. `html.typed-ready` (added by `initTypedFocus`) reveals the caret and the pause control; without it the first phrase is a complete static line. Never add a page-wide scroll-reveal system, parallax on reading matter, count-up metrics or perpetual animation. The only scroll-linked motion is the header progress rule, the CV spine (`view()`), the storymap rail and the V13 hero scene (decorative ink only, 64/48/16px over the first 90vh); the copy, index and profile band never move. The only `infinite` animations are the caret blink (removed when rotation settles) and the submit sweep, which exists only while `is-sending` and is not applied under reduced motion.
- **Visual system (V7–V11):** one accent (`--action`, ultramarine) on a white canvas; `--surface-paper` marks alternating bands, the feature plates, the contact brief and hover, `--surface-bed` sits behind media, `--surface-tint` is the single award-note surface. Serif is limited to the wordmark, `h1`, `h2` and the two editorial `h3` roles — on all three pages, so both feature plates' `h2` and the two storymaps' `h1`/`h2` qualify and nothing else does (chapter numerals, the plates' three counts and the storymaps' stat counts are sans, tabular). Surfaces are square (`--radius: 0`), tags use `--radius-sm` (2px), controls are pills (`--radius-pill`), and the portrait, eyebrow node, nav toggle, typed toggle and register arrows are circles. Section heads are a two-column grid above 1120px (`"eyebrow eyebrow" / "title intro"`, 0.57/0.43, baseline-aligned) and collapse to a block at ≤1120px. The header has no shadow: scrolling deepens only its bottom rule. The footer is an ultramarine field with a white serif wordmark and a periwinkle graph — it stays the page's only full-bleed colour field; the feature plates are paper on purpose. Nav current/hover stays weight 500 so scrollspy cannot shift layout; blue plus the underline carry the state.
- **Hero typed focus line:** only `.hero__focus-line` rotates; `h1` stays static. The visible span is always `aria-hidden` and one `.visually-hidden` sentence carries the same claim for assistive technology. `initTypedFocus()` reads the other phrases from `<script type="application/json" data-typed-phrases>`, reserves the tallest phrase height on `.hero__focus-line` before clearing the span, types at 26 ms (140 ms after `[.,;:—–·]`), holds 6 s, erases at 12 ms and advances. It never simulates mistakes and never loops without the control. Rotation stops when the tab is hidden, the hero is off screen (one `IntersectionObserver` on `#top`), reduced motion is requested, or the user pauses; the pause control completes the current phrase and swaps its label/icon. Reduced motion, print and a module failure all leave phrase 1 as a complete static line with no caret and no button.
- **Arrival acknowledgement:** `initArrival()` delegates one `click` listener for fragment links inside `.nav`, `.footer-nav`, `.hero__actions` and `.hero__index`, adds `.is-arrived` to the destination section for 900 ms, and forces a reflow so a repeated click restarts the 700 ms rule draw under the `h2`. If the target is off screen it waits for one `scrollend` (900 ms fallback) so the smooth scroll does not spend the draw; that listener is transient, not scroll math. It never moves focus, never runs on load, the skip link or reduced motion, and removes its own class.
- **Copy e-mail:** `initCopyEmail()` reveals the button, uses `navigator.clipboard.writeText` with a hidden-textarea `execCommand` fallback, swaps Copy → Copied with a drawn check for 2 s, and never replaces the `mailto:` link.
- **Scroll-drawn CV spine:** `.cv-list::before` fills and `.cv-entry::before` markers resolve from rule grey to action blue with `animation-timeline: view()` only, inside `@supports` and `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. Without support, on ≤900px, in print and under reduced motion the spine is a complete static rule.
- **Footer graph:** a 9-node/10-edge inline SVG echo of the retired hero field, `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`, drawn once by the shared rule observer, hidden at ≤900px, in print and in forced colours. It is periwinkle (`--tint`) at 0.45 opacity on the ultramarine ground. It must never change footer dimensions.
- **Hero index:** `nav.hero__index` is the second and last child of `.hero__grid`, `aria-label="Explore the work"`, with a `.hero__index-bar` label `Index` and an `aria-hidden` range `01–04`, and a `.hero__index-grid` of four `a.hero__index-link` — 01 Work → `#portfolio-section`, 02 Storymaps → `storymap.html`, 03 Training → `#teaching-section`, 04 Experience → `#cv-section`. Each pane carries a white line glyph (`#i-stack`, `#i-pin`, `#i-cap`, `#i-briefcase`) at 40px, an `aria-hidden` ordinal, a title + arrow and one factual meta (`21 projects`, `DERLA · MEMOR`, `Workshops · talks`, `Since 2009`). A solid `--action` field, `--on-dark*` ink, `--action-hover` on hover/focus, a 1px sash from the grid gap and a 2px white focus ring inside each pane. Pane `min-height` is 148px, 118px at ≤640 and 112px at ≤420.
