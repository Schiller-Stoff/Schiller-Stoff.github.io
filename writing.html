02

How I chose

Reach, then one argument each.

The ranking starts with the record: 3,154 downloads for the agent workshop, 862 for the Copilot workshop, then a long tail at 227, 140 and 103. These five were chosen from the Zenodo records cited across this site.

Downloads are not a measure of quality. They are a measure of use. A workshop that 2,760 people downloaded has been read in rooms I will never enter.

The second rule was distinctness. Three further AI-coding workshops — 264, 260 and 251 unique downloads — repeat the first two arguments. They remain in the record, and this series does not reprint them.

The rule is in public because a selection without a rule is a preference.
